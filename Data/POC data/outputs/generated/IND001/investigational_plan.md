# 1.20 General Investigational Plan - POC Draft

Case: IND001

## Rationale for the drug and the research
AVC-101 is an oral small-molecule inhibitor intended to address the limited treatment options for participants with recurrent low-grade serous ovarian cancer, a tumour type that responds poorly to conventional cytotoxic chemotherapy. Nonclinical studies support an acceptable margin between the pharmacologically active exposure and exposures associated with toxicity.

## Indication to be studied
recurrent low-grade serous ovarian cancer

## General approach to evaluating the drug
Development will proceed sequentially. A first-in-human dose-escalation study will characterise safety, tolerability and pharmacokinetics and identify a recommended dose. Expansion at that dose will provide a preliminary assessment of antitumour activity before any decision to proceed to a randomised study.

## Kinds of clinical trials planned for the first year
One Phase 1 dose-escalation study with a dose-expansion component is planned in the first year following submission. No additional clinical studies are planned during that period.

## Estimated number of participants
Approximately 36 participants (up to 24 in dose escalation and 12 in dose expansion).

## Anticipated risks of particular severity or seriousness
Based on repeat-dose toxicology in rat and dog, the anticipated risks of particular severity are reversible hepatocellular injury observed at exposures above the proposed starting dose, and dose-dependent neutropenia. Liver function tests and absolute neutrophil counts are protocol-specified stopping criteria.

## Studies planned for the first year

### 1. A Phase 1 Study of AVC-101 in Participants with Recurrent Low-Grade Serous Ovarian Cancer
- **Objectives:** To determine the safety, tolerability and maximum tolerated dose of oral AVC-101; to characterise single- and multiple-dose pharmacokinetics; to make a preliminary assessment of antitumour activity.
- **Study design:** Open-label, single-arm, 3+3 dose escalation followed by dose expansion at the recommended dose.
- **Planned sample size:** Up to 36 participants; no formal sample size calculation was performed for this preliminary study.
- **Study population:** Adults with histologically confirmed recurrent low-grade serous ovarian cancer who have received at least one prior line of systemic therapy.
- **Study parameters:** Safety and tolerability by adverse events, laboratory tests, ECG and physical examination. Pharmacokinetic parameters include Cmax, Tmax and AUC. Antitumour activity assessed by RECIST v1.1.
- **Status:** Protocol submitted with this Initial IND. Enrolment has not started.
