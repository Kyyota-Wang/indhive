# 1.20 General Investigational Plan - POC Draft

Case: IND005

## Rationale for the drug and the research
CNV-512 is an intravenous agent intended to improve exercise capacity in heart failure with preserved ejection fraction, a condition with no approved disease-modifying therapy. A prior Phase 1 study in healthy volunteers established tolerability across the dose range now proposed.

## Indication to be studied
heart failure with preserved ejection fraction

## General approach to evaluating the drug
Development moves directly to a Phase 2a study in the target population, using exercise capacity and haemodynamic endpoints to establish proof of mechanism before a larger efficacy study is designed.

## Kinds of clinical trials planned for the first year
One Phase 2a study in adults with heart failure with preserved ejection fraction is planned in the first year following submission.

## Estimated number of participants
Approximately 60 participants randomised across two dose levels and placebo.

## Anticipated risks of particular severity or seriousness
Anticipated risks of particular severity are symptomatic hypotension during and shortly after infusion, and renal function decline observed at supratherapeutic exposures in the 26-week rat study. Blood pressure is monitored throughout infusion and creatinine is a protocol-specified stopping criterion.

## Studies planned for the first year

### 1. A Phase 2a Study of CNV-512 in Adults with Heart Failure with Preserved Ejection Fraction
- **Objectives:** To assess the effect of CNV-512 on exercise capacity and to evaluate safety and tolerability in the target population.
- **Study design:** Randomised, double-blind, placebo-controlled, parallel-group study with two active dose levels.
- **Planned sample size:** Approximately 60 participants, 20 per arm.
- **Study population:** Adults with heart failure with preserved ejection fraction, NYHA class II to III, with an ejection fraction of at least 50 percent.
- **Study parameters:** Primary endpoint is change in six-minute walk distance. Safety assessed by adverse events, blood pressure, renal function and ECG.
- **Status:** Protocol submitted with this Initial IND. Enrolment has not started.
