# 1.20 General Investigational Plan - POC Draft

Case: IND004

## Rationale for the drug and the research
RNV-220 is an inhaled agent intended to provide once-daily control of moderate persistent asthma with lower systemic exposure than currently available oral therapy. Nonclinical inhalation toxicology supports evaluation in humans at the proposed doses.

## Indication to be studied
moderate persistent asthma

## General approach to evaluating the drug
A first-in-human study in healthy adult volunteers will establish safety, tolerability and systemic exposure following single and repeat inhaled doses before any study is conducted in participants with asthma.

## Kinds of clinical trials planned for the first year
One Phase 1 single- and multiple-ascending-dose study in healthy adult volunteers is planned in the first year. A patient study is contemplated thereafter but its protocol has not been developed.

## Estimated number of participants
Approximately 48 healthy adult volunteers across all dose cohorts.

## Anticipated risks of particular severity or seriousness
Anticipated risks of particular severity are bronchospasm following inhaled administration and, at the highest planned doses, the systemic corticosteroid-like effects seen in 13-week dog inhalation toxicology. Spirometry is performed before and after each dose, and rescue medication is available at the site.

## Studies planned for the first year

### 1. A Phase 1 Study of RNV-220 in Healthy Adult Volunteers
- **Objectives:** To evaluate the safety and tolerability of single and multiple ascending inhaled doses of RNV-220 and to characterise systemic pharmacokinetics.
- **Study design:** Randomised, double-blind, placebo-controlled, single- and multiple-ascending-dose study.
- **Planned sample size:** Approximately 48 participants, 8 per cohort randomised 6:2 active to placebo.
- **Study population:** Healthy adults aged 18 to 55 with normal spirometry and no history of reactive airway disease.
- **Study parameters:** Adverse events, spirometry, vital signs, ECG, clinical laboratory tests, and plasma pharmacokinetics.
- **Status:** Protocol submitted with this Initial IND. Enrolment has not started.
